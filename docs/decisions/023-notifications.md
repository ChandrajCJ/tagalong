# 023: Notifications you control, reminders, and gentler places

**Status:** accepted

## Decision
- **Six kinds of push**, each switchable: chat (bundled new messages), @mentions (immediate), plan (stops added, moved to another day, removed), money (an expense you share, "you owe ₹640"; someone paid you), to-dos (one put on you; yours due tomorrow) and trip (its name or dates changed). Never to whoever made the change, and never to someone with the trip open right now (presence).
- **Settings live in `identity.notification_prefs`.** The row with no trip holds a person's defaults (Profile → Notifications); a row per trip overrides them (trip ⋯ → Notifications), where null means "as my defaults". No row means everything on. `muted_until` mutes a trip (or everything, on the defaults row) for a day, a week, or "until I turn it back on" (year 9999). Muting stops everything except @mentions.
- **One pipeline for plan, money, to-dos and trip.** Services call a small notifier (`lib/notifier.ts`) after the change commits. It writes a short line per recipient to Redis (`activity:<kind>:<trip>:<user>`) and queues one job per person, trip and kind with the same 20 s wait as chat, so a burst becomes one push: "Sam changed Day 2: 3 updates". The worker checks prefs, mute and presence when it sends, so a setting changed during the wait still counts. The notifier never throws: a notification is never worth failing the change for.
- **Due tomorrow** is an hourly job. It sends the first run after 9 am on the trip's clock (its time zone, else UTC), once per to-do and due date (a Redis key), and only for open to-dos with someone on them.
- **Taps open the right screen:** chat, the day in Plan (`/plan?day=…`), Money, Lists, the trip, or the stop.
- **Android channels** (Chat, Mentions, Plan & money, Reminders) are created from JavaScript; each push names its channel, so people can also tune them in system settings.
- **Reminders are local notifications**, scheduled on the phone by `expo-notifications`, so they're free and work offline. A pure function in `packages/shared` (`wantedReminders`, `diffReminders`) turns the next 7 days of timed stops and bookings into reminders. It uses the trip's time zone, gives flights at least 3 h, lets a booking on a plan item replace the item's reminder, and keys each reminder by stop, start time and lead, so a moved stop cancels its old one. The phone re-syncs on opening, on coming back to the app, and after plan or booking events. The lead time (15 min, 30 min, 1 h or 2 h; default 30 min) is stored with the defaults so it follows the person between phones. The web app has none and says so.
- **Places, gently.** In the Plan list, a stop with no location gets a quiet "Not on the map yet · Add place" under it, or "Is this Fort Aguada? Yes / Pick another" when its text matches exactly one place near the trip. The ✕ means "no place needed" (`items.place_hint_dismissed`). Stops of type "other" never get one. When two or more of a day's stops lack a place, the day says so. The idea sheet's "Where" uses the same place search, so ideas land on the Plan map and keep their place when moved into the plan; an idea for a place that's already an idea reuses it.
- **Explore's food, nightlife and shopping no longer come back empty.** For a region, Overpass is asked about up to four small boxes around the busiest spots (the centre and the best-known Wikipedia places), not the whole state, with a 15 s server timeout and at most 80 results. Photon is asked too, by OpenStreetMap tag (restaurants, cafés, bars, malls, markets…), and everything is merged. If every source fails, the API answers 503 "Couldn't load places right now" and the app offers a retry. Failures are logged by source. A partial answer is kept for 10 minutes and quietly asked again in the background.

## Limits today
- Reminders need notification permission, which the app asks for alongside pushes; Expo Go can't show them reliably.
- Reminders are rescheduled while the app runs; a stop changed while the phone is off updates the next time it's opened.
- "Day 2" in plan pushes comes from the trip's start date; a stop with no day says "Anytime".
