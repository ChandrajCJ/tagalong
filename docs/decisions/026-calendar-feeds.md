# 026: Calendar feeds, countdown pushes and profile photos

**Status:** accepted

## Decision
- **A calendar link per person, where the token is the key.** `GET /calendar/<token>.ics` needs no sign-in, because Google and Apple Calendar can't sign in. The token is 32 random bytes; only its SHA-256 is kept, in `identity.calendar_feeds`, one row per person, as with invites. Making a new link replaces the hash, so the old link 404s at once, and **Stop sharing** deletes the row. An unknown, replaced or malformed token is a plain 404. `last_fetched_at` shows the person when a calendar last read it.
- **The phone remembers the link, the server can't.** Since only the hash is stored, Profile → Calendar keeps the full link on the device that made it (secure storage) for **Add to Google Calendar** (Google's `render?cid=` page with the `webcal://` address), **Add to Apple or another calendar** (`webcal://`) and **Copy link**. On another device it says the link is on, and offers a new one.
- **What's in it:** every trip I'm on now (leaving or being removed takes it out) as an all-day span; every timed plan stop, on its day's place's clock (`timezoneForStop`); and every booking at its exact instant in UTC, with stays as separate check-in and check-out events. A plan item that has a booking is left out, since the booking already covers it. Stops without a time come in as all-day items only when the person turns that on.
- **A small RFC 5545 writer in shared (`ics.ts`), no dependency.** UIDs are stable (`item-<id>@tagalong`, `booking-<id>[-in|-out]@tagalong`, `trip-<id>@tagalong`) and `SEQUENCE` is the row's version, so a subscribed calendar replaces an event when it changes. Text is escaped, lines are folded at 75 bytes without splitting a character, and each zone gets a `VTIMEZONE` listing its actual offset changes over the events' years. Those changes are found from `Intl` (checked every six hours, then pinned to the minute) rather than hand-written rules.
- **Events link back to the web app** through `APP_URL`, or the first `CORS_ORIGINS` entry when that's unset; without either, the links are left out.
- **One trip as a file:** `GET /trips/:id/calendar.ics` for members, from the trip's ⋯ menu. The browser saves it; the phone hands it to the share sheet.
- **Countdown pushes are a notification kind (`countdown`, on by default)**, so the existing defaults, per-trip overrides and mute all apply. An hourly job (`jobs/countdown.ts`) sends four stages: a week out (only if a to-do or packing item is open), three days out, 7 pm the evening before (with that day's place's forecast from Open-Meteo), and 8 am on the day (the first booking or timed stop). Times are on the person's own clock: phones send their time zone with the push token, and the trip's zone is the fallback. Each stage is claimed in `identity.countdown_sent` before it's sent, so it goes once even when two runs overlap. The wording is pure (`countdownMessage` in shared).
- **"Get ready" counts are one query (`lib/readiness.ts`)**: my own ticks on "everyone" lines plus shared lines' ticks. The trips list, the Overview and the countdown all use it.
- **Profile photos are cropped on the phone and shown only to co-members.** The phone crops square, makes it 512 px and saves it as JPEG. The upload goes straight to storage at `avatars/<user>/<random>.jpg`, presigned for JPEG, PNG or WebP only. The server checks on confirm that the key is the person's own and that the object is at most 2 MB (a bigger file is deleted), then deletes the previous photo. The trip payload carries each member's short-lived link (an hour) and an `avatarVersion` taken from the key. The app's `<Avatar>` caches by that version, so photos don't reload on every screen but do change when replaced; initials stay the fallback. `GET /users/:id/avatar` redirects to the photo for yourself and people who share a current trip, and 404s for everyone else.

## Limits today
- A calendar app polls on its own schedule (Google: every few hours), so changes are slow to appear there.
- A calendar link made on one device can't be shown on another; making a new one there replaces it everywhere.
- Countdown pushes reach phones with the app's push token; the web app gets none.
