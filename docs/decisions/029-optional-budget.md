# 029: An optional budget

**Status:** accepted

## Decision
- **Optional at every step.** A trip has at most one row in `expenses.budgets` (keyed by `trip_id`), and most trips have none. With none, `GET /trips/:id/money` returns `budget: null` and `planned: []`, no alerts or events are produced, and the phone shows only a quiet "Add a budget (optional)" link on Money (editors and owners). A total with no categories, categories with no total, or both are all valid; a budget with neither is refused (400).
- **Group or each person.** `mode` is `group` (amounts for the whole trip) or `per_person` (every amount, total and category limits, is per person). In per-person mode each person's card measures their own share of each expense; planned costs are shared out by headcount.
- **Writes.** `PUT /trips/:id/budget {mode, totalMinor, categories, version?}` adds (no `version`, 201) or changes (the version last seen, 200); a mismatch, or adding when one exists, is 409 with the current copy. `DELETE /trips/:id/budget` removes it and its alerts. Editors and owners only; viewers see it. Every write logs `change_log` entity `budget` in the same transaction and publishes `budget.updated` (payload: the budget, or null once removed) after commit. New trip accepts `budget: {mode, totalMinor}` and Edit trip `budget: {mode, totalMinor | null} | null`: a null total keeps category limits, and with none left the budget is removed.
- **The summary is worked out on the phone,** like balances: `budgetSummary` in `@tagalong/shared` takes the budget, the expenses (already in the trip's currency at their frozen rate), the planned costs, the trip's dates and headcount, today and me. It gives spent, planned, left, a per-day allowance (what's left after planned, over the trip days still to come), and from day 2 of the trip a pace projection: spent so far plus the in-trip daily rate times the days after today. So live `expense.*` events update the card at once. The Overview gets a light `GET /trips/:id/budget` (budget, spent, my share) for "Budget: ₹6,600 left".
- **Planned, never counted twice.** Planned costs are plan item estimates (per person, so times the people on the trip) and booking prices, converted at today's rate (a currency with no rate is left out, not guessed). A booking with a price stands in for its item's estimate. An expense linked to a plan item covers the item and its bookings; one linked to a booking covers that booking (`unlinkedPlanned`). "Add as expense" on the item screen already links them.
- **Chat cards at 80% and 100%, once each.** After every expense create or edit and every budget change, `checkBudgetAlerts` compares the whole group's spending with the group limit (the total, times the headcount in per-person mode) and inserts the reached thresholds into `expenses.budget_alerts (trip_id, threshold)` with `on conflict do nothing`; only rows actually inserted post a card, so two writes at once can't both post. Jumping past both marks posts only "Budget reached". Raising the budget (or removing it) deletes alert rows that are no longer reached, so they fire again.

## Limits today
- Category limits have no chat alerts; only the total does.
- The per-person limit for alerts uses today's headcount, so someone joining later raises it.
- Planned costs in a foreign currency move with today's rate until they're paid.
